OUR STORY / 关于我们

Wabi-Sabi in the Heart of Nairobi / 内罗毕的侘寂美学

Yeza Inn 悦在民宿 is a boutique aparthotel nestled within Kindaruma Homes Apartments, Kilimani. Opened in 2025 and built for one purpose: to give business travellers venturing to Africa a serene, home-like sanctuary. With trilingual staff (Chinese, English, Swahili), a Chinese restaurant, and 9 carefully designed rooms, Yeza Inn blends the quiet intentionality of Wabi-Sabi with Kenyan warmth.

悦在民宿坐落于内罗毕基里马尼区，2025年开业，专为赴非洲出行的商务旅行者打造。我们提供中英斯三语管家服务、中式餐饮及9间精心设计的客房，将侘寂美学融入肯尼亚的温暖之中。
